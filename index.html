How to play
  1. Moles pop out of the holes - tap one to whack it.
  2. Normal mole: +10 points. Golden mole: +50 points, but it is quick!
  3. Never whack a bomb: -25 points and your combo breaks.
  4. Consecutive hits build a combo multiplier up to x5.
  5. In Endless mode you have 3 lives - a mole that escapes or a bomb costs one life.
